Official intake channel

Send a document to NITDA and follow it to a decision.

Letters, applications, filings, reports and proposals addressed to the Agency are logged in the central registry the moment they arrive. You receive a tracking reference, an acknowledgement target measured in working days, and a timeline you can read at any time.

This portal is for correspondence addressed to NITDA. It is not a staff system and carries no internal case handling — those functions live in the Agency's operations platform, which remains the system of record.
