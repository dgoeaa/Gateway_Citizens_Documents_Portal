Privacy notice

How this portal uses your information.

Technical data-handling notice for this pre-production package. It is not an approved organizational privacy policy or a statement of legal compliance.

The deployment owner must approve the controller, lawful basis, retention and contact information before collecting live citizen data.

Who is responsible

The supplied source identifies NITDA and gives dpo@nitda.gov.ng as a contact. These details and the deployment owner's authority have not been live-validated. Do not submit real personal data until the owner has approved this deployment.

What we collect, and when

  • When you submit a document: your full name, official email address, organisation and organisation type; your telephone number and state, if you give them; the type of correspondence and its priority; the subject and the purpose you write; and the files you attach, with the file names you sent.
  • When you confirm your email address: the address, and a one-time code we email to it. The code expires and can be used once.
  • When you track a request: the tracking ID and a valid email-verification proof. Knowing an email address and reference is not sufficient.
  • When you open a helpdesk case: the topic, your name, your email address, the tracking ID if you give one, and your message.
  • When you respond on a request: the response or note you send, attached to that request.

The portal does not ask for your national identity number, bank details or date of birth, and you do not create an account.

Why we use it

  • To receive, register and route your correspondence to the unit that handles it, and to reply to you.
  • To publish recorded status changes and support replies through verified lookup. Only verification codes are emailed by this package.
  • To confirm that you control the email address before we accept a submission or show you a record.
  • To answer helpdesk cases.
  • To keep the service secure and working: each request to the Agency's systems leaves a run record, which is used to detect failures and misuse.

The operator must establish and publish a lawful basis and procedures for data-subject requests before live use. A checkbox in this package does not establish legal compliance.

Where it is kept, and who sees it

Records, support cases, ratings, audit events and hashed verification credentials are stored in the server's local SQLite database. Attachment bytes are private files outside the public web directory. Mail transport receives the recipient address and code. No SharePoint or Microsoft 365 connection is implemented.

A verified submitter can read their own records and cases. All operator emails in the server-managed allowlist can read the registry, attachments, feedback and audit events; per-directorate staff isolation is not implemented.

How long we keep it

Record deletion and retention automation are not implemented. The operator must establish a retention schedule and secure deletion process. Codes expire after 10 minutes and are single-use; verification proofs expire after 30 minutes. Expiry blocks access, but does not itself securely erase backups or old database pages.

What is stored on your device

This browser stores draft answers, local preparation records, tracking IDs, cached cases/replies and theme choices. Attachments are not restored with a saved draft: reattach the original files. Browser data is not encrypted by this application. Avoid shared devices and clear this site's data after use.

Verification proofs are held only in page memory and expire server-side. There are no application cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party runtime scripts. The service worker caches only public shell assets, never API responses or staff data. Failed creates are not resent automatically.

Your rights

The source material lists the following data-subject requests. The deployment owner must approve contacts and fulfillment procedures; this package does not automate them:

  • tell you whether we hold data about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, or restrict how we use it, where the law allows;
  • give you your data in a commonly used electronic format;
  • stop using your data where you object to it;
  • stop relying on your consent, where consent is the basis.

Use a verified support case to contact this deployment's operators. Confirm official privacy contacts independently before sending sensitive information.

Keeping it secure

Production configuration requires an HTTPS origin and an operator-managed TLS reverse proxy. Local test mode is unencrypted loopback HTTP and writes codes to a private test file instead of emailing them. Email verification is mandatory for records, support and feedback. Upload tickets enforce declared size, extension and SHA-256 integrity. Files are not malware-scanned; a filename extension is not proof of safe content.

Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
